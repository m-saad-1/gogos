Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept meal vouchers?

Yes, we accept major meal vouchers. Ask our staff for the exact accepted providers when you arrive.

Does the restaurant have a kids area?

Yes, we have a recreational area with monitors on weekends. Your kids can safely play while you enjoy your meal!

Do you offer gluten-free or vegan options?

Absolutely! We have a specialized vegan menu and can customize most of our burgers with a gluten-free bun or a lettuce wrap upon request.

Can I book a table for a large party or event?

Yes, we accept reservations for large groups. Please head over to the Reservations page or call us directly to discuss the details for your event.

Do you offer delivery services?

Yes, we deliver within a 5-mile radius. You can order directly through our website or find us on your favorite food delivery apps.
