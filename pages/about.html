About Us

Tradition and Authentic Flavor

Our Story

Takeaway was born out of a passion for authentic culinary experiences. From our humble beginnings, we have been dedicated to serving the finest fast food and flame-grilled delights. Every recipe is crafted with a secret blend of herbs and spices, ensuring an explosion of flavor in every bite.

We believe that food brings people together. That's why we create a welcoming environment, use only the freshest locally sourced ingredients, and ensure our staff provides warm, prompt hospitality.

Why Choose Us

100% Fresh & Safe

We work with trusted local suppliers daily to bring you pure, premium meat and farm-fresh vegetables under strict hygiene controls.

Flaming Taste

Our grill masters cook items over open flames, locking in juices and providing a signature smoky aroma that keeps guests coming back.

Delivery & Takeaway Services

How we bring delicious food straight to you, on time and fresh.

Home Delivery

  • Estimated Delivery Time: 30 to 45 minutes depending on traffic and order volume.
  • Delivery Hours: Everyday from 11:30 AM to 11:30 PM.
  • Coverage Area: Delivery service is available within an 8km radius of DHA Karachi.
  • Delivery Rates: Flat Rs 100 delivery fee. Free delivery on all orders above Rs 1000!

Takeaway & Pick-up

  • Estimated Prep Time: Just 15 to 20 minutes from the time you order online.
  • Takeaway Hours: Everyday from 11:00 AM to 12:00 AM.
  • How it Works: Complete your order online, choose the "Takeaway" option, skip the lines and pick up at the express counter!
  • Deals: Get an extra 5% discount on checkout for selecting the takeaway option!
